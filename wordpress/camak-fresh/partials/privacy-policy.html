Information about the enquiry and privacy controls available on this redesigned website.

Contacting Camak

When you make an enquiry, the contact form asks for your name, email address, location and cleaning requirements. Your phone number is optional. Please only include information needed to discuss your cleaning request.

How your enquiry is sent

The form sends your enquiry to Camak’s email address using the website’s configured email service. The theme does not create a separate enquiry database. The hosting provider or email service may retain technical logs and delivered messages. A temporary, hashed network identifier is used to limit repeated form submissions.

Privacy preferences

This redesign does not add analytics or advertising trackers. It stores your privacy preference in your browser when you save it. You can reopen privacy preferences from the footer or clear your browser’s site data.

External services

WhatsApp, Google Maps and email links open external services with their own privacy practices. The website’s hosting service may also process technical access information to serve and secure the website.

Questions about your information

For Camak’s current privacy policy, or to ask about information you have shared with the business, contact info@camakservices.co.nz or call +64 27 296 0929.
